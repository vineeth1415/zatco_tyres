Optimizing Fleet Uptime in High-Altitude Copper Mining

Location location_on Atacama, Chile
Product Used tire_repair Earthmover XL
Key Result trending_up 15% Uptime Increase

warning The Environment

Operating at an elevation of over 4,000 meters in the Atacama Desert, the extraction facility faces some of the most brutal environmental conditions on the planet. The terrain is dominated by highly abrasive volcanic rock, which significantly accelerates tread wear and increases the risk of catastrophic carcass failure.

Furthermore, the extreme temperature fluctuations—ranging from sub-zero nights to scorching daytime ground temperatures—stress standard rubber compounds beyond their operational limits. The existing tire inventory was experiencing a high rate of premature scrap, directly impacting fleet availability and ton-per-hour output.

precision_manufacturing The ZATCO Solution

ZATCO engineering teams conducted a comprehensive site audit, analyzing wear patterns, haul road profiles, and operational cycles. The resulting recommendation was the deployment of the Earthmover XL series, specifically configured for high-abrasion environments.

  • layers
    Proprietary Compound Z-74 A highly cut-resistant synthetic blend designed specifically to withstand the tearing forces of jagged volcanic rock while maintaining thermal stability under heavy loads.
  • settings_backup_restore
    Reinforced Bead Construction Engineered with high-tensile steel cord architecture to handle the extreme lateral forces exerted during tight cornering on steep inclines, minimizing bead chafing and rim slip.
